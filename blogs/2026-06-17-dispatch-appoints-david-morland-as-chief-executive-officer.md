Longtime CFO to Lead Dispatch into Its Next Phase of Growth Dispatch announces the appointment of David Morland as Chief Executive Officer. David succeeds Pat Burns, who is leaving the company to pursue a new professional opportunity. Since joining Dispatch in 2016, David has played a pivotal role in the company’s growth and evolution.
